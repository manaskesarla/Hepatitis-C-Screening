# Field interaction record

**Status:** Planned. No consultation is recorded yet.

Ask a staff member at a blood bank or diagnostic laboratory for a short conversation about the existing reporting and follow-up process. Ask permission before taking notes. Record only information the organization agrees to share; omit donor and patient details.

## Questions

1. Which types of visits or donations involve laboratory panels? Which of ALB, ALP, ALT, AST, BIL, CHE, CHOL, CREA, GGT and PROT are routinely available, and in which units?
2. When a value is abnormal, who reviews it, who communicates it, and how does the person receive it?
3. If a donor or patient cannot be reached, who owns follow-up and how is it documented?
4. What hepatitis testing or referral already occurs when indicated? How does that process differ between donor screening and diagnostic care?
5. In a classroom demonstration, what wording would distinguish a model flag from a result that can guide care?
